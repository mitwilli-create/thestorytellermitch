executive-voice-agent

A VP's voice, built as a system.

High-stakes executive drafts were taking too many rounds to sound like the principal they served. I built a private Gemini drafting partner around a written voice constitution, audience modes, risk pressure tests, and a banned-phrase check. The principal kept final sign-off.

The brief

More than 1,000 senior engineers formed the audience. Ranging from recurring updates to high-stakes change communications, the work demanded a system that preserved a principal's judgment and cadence while making a first draft faster to review.

The source material and raw instructions remain private. This page describes the system boundary and the decisions it encoded, with the public email and delivered-address samples as output evidence.

The operating rules

Match the audience. Team updates used direct empathy and addressed the difficult point early. Senior-leader updates used concise narrative logic. Broadcast remarks stayed grounded and moved from structural limits to individual agency.

Keep the truth visible. The system favored plain language, named friction, and rejected corporate filler. It treated a qualified answer as stronger than false certainty.

Make the boundary explicit. The agent could draft and pressure-test. It could not approve, publish, or invent a commitment. The human principal remained the decision-maker.
