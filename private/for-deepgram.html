The editorial job

Find the human moment inside the technology, report it honestly, and make the proof useful to the person deciding whether to build or buy.

01 · Make it heard

Start where trust breaks.

Flux is Deepgram’s text to speech model for live voice agents. When a caller cuts in, Flux reports what the caller heard and what the model did not finish, so the agent can continue from the right place instead of repeating itself.

The test

A caller interrupts to correct an account number. Does the agent adjust, or repeat a sentence the caller already heard?

01 · Name itLead with the interruption.

Make the human problem clear before introducing model language.

02 · Prove itLet people hear the difference.

Use the same script, settings, and disclosed method in a controlled comparison.

03 · Direct itGive each reader a next step.

Send buyers to operational proof and builders to the test method.

What I would change in the launch

Keep: the product, the founder voice, the human scene, and the technical audience. Add: the interruption as the opening argument, audio as the proof, enterprise buyers as the tiebreaker, and a separate action for every audience.

Audience rule

Enterprise buyers set the priority. Builders get the method. Press gets the consequence. General readers get the human moment. The page and distribution change. The facts do not.

03 · Make it ship

Ship the proof. Then build the franchise.

The first 90 days should produce one or two finished packages and a practical editorial framework for the next 6 to 12 months.

First 30 daysSecure the access and evidence.

Confirm customer access, partner permissions, available experts, approved demonstrations, the enterprise reader action, and the measure for each package.

By 60 daysShip the interruption package.

Publish the story, controlled audio, short video, executive adaptation, and builder fixture. Distribute buyer and builder versions separately.

By 90 daysPublish the enterprise trust story.

Use public evidence if partner interviews are delayed. Begin the restaurant story only if named access is confirmed.

Six to 12 monthsBuild the human voice benchmark.

Publish the method, sample files, scoring system, limitations, and a recurring scorecard that can show weak results as well as gains.

The longer bet

What makes a voice sound human?

Turn Deepgram’s Audio Turing Test ambition into a transparent editorial benchmark. Measure tone, timing, interruption handling, emotional context, and consistency. Call it a Deepgram editorial benchmark, not an established scientific standard.

Written

Essay and method.

Audio and video

One quality at a time, with an engineer and editor explaining what listeners heard.

Builder

Prompts, sample files, scoring method, limitations, and code.

What editorial owns

Story mining, reporting, commissioning, editing, audience versions, production standards, the calendar, and the learning loop. Communications, Developer Relations, Growth, and Sales remain partners in distribution and conversion.

What needs an internal answer

Which customers can be named? Which executives, engineers, and partners are available? What has already been pitched? Which demonstrations and audio can be published?

What I would not assume

I would not imply customer access, partner approval, or a media relationship that has not been confirmed. I would disclose comparison methods and separate paid distribution from organic response.
