03 · Make it ship

Ship the proof.Then build from what it teaches.

The three packages follow a deliberate order: interruption first, enterprise trust second, and restaurant proof only when access is real. The work above becomes the 90-day plan below.

Why this sequence

Lead: the interruption package gives buyers a human proof point and builders a test. Then: the enterprise story answers the deployment questions. Later: the benchmark turns the best test into a repeatable editorial franchise.

First 30 daysSecure the access and evidence.

Confirm customer access, partner permissions, available experts, approved demonstrations, the enterprise reader action, and the measure for each package.

By 60 daysShip the interruption package.

Publish the story, controlled audio, short video, and builder fixture. Distribute buyer and builder versions separately.

By 90 daysPublish the enterprise trust story.

Use public evidence if partner interviews are delayed. Begin the restaurant story only if named access is confirmed.

Six to 12 monthsBuild the human voice benchmark.

Publish the method, sample files, scoring system, limitations, and a recurring scorecard that can show weak results as well as gains.

The longer bet

Make the test repeatable.

Turn the interruption test into a transparent editorial benchmark. Measure tone, timing, interruption handling, emotional context, and consistency. Call it a Deepgram editorial benchmark, not an established scientific standard.

Written

Essay and method that explain what makes a voice sound human.

Audio and video

One quality at a time, with an engineer and editor explaining what listeners heard.

Builder

Prompts, sample files, scoring method, limitations, and code.

Before production

Resolve the few facts that determine the package: which customers can be named, which experts and partners are available, what has already been pitched, and which demonstrations can be published. These are reporting inputs, not a catch-all appendix.
