The daily workflow

Communications Operator Guide · Retained excerpt

Morning routine (15 minutes)

  1. Check [notifications] for escalations. High Touch alerts: handle first. Medium Touch: add to today's queue. System errors: check logs, troubleshoot.
  2. Review [the tracking sheet]. Sort by Status: “Pending Review.” Check Touch Level column for Medium/High. Verify Doc Link column has Starter Docs loaded.
  3. Triage your day. High Touch with deadlines <3 days: priority. Medium Touch: batch process (3-5 at once). Low Touch flags (if any): quick spot-check.

During the day

For each Medium/High Touch request:

  1. Open the Starter Doc.
  2. Read the Summary: What does the requester actually need?
  3. Check [audience research]: Is there audience intel?
  4. Validate Classification: Does Medium/High seem right?
  5. Build the deliverable. Medium: strategy playbook. High: schedule discovery call, begin drafting.
  6. Update [the tracking sheet]. Status: “In Progress” (while working). Status: “Completed” (when delivered). Add deliverable link to Doc Link column.

Evening wrap-up (10 minutes)

  • Clear “In Progress” items. Finish or move to tomorrow with notes.
  • Respond to any same-day requesters, even if just “Working on it, expect delivery tomorrow.”
  • Check for new High Touch escalations. Set up for next morning.

The weekly workflow

Monday morning

  • Review the week's Medium/High Touch pipeline.
  • Flag any requests with tight deadlines.
  • Batch similar requests (e.g., 3 mandate playbooks can share structure).

Friday afternoon

  • Complete all “In Progress” items or document handoff state.
  • Archive completed Low Touch requests (status = “Completed” and >1 week old).
  • Check for any orphaned requests (no status update in 5+ days).

When the classification is wrong

Issue: Classification seems wrong (e.g., High Touch but looks routine).

Fix: Trust your judgment. Reclassify in the sheet, notify requester of new timeline, document reasoning.
