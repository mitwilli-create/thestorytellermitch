The redesign brief

Selected passages from the project charter. It names Mitchell Williams as author and project owner, with a January 28, 2019 header and later 2019 revision entries. Goals below are goals, not a claim that each outcome was measured.

Objectives

Establish and develop a relationship with Noogler orientation team to better understand where content can be updated and how it is being used in both regional onboarding hubs and local offices.

Create a consistent Techstop Noogler orientation experience across all sites, both hub and non-hub onboarding

Reflect an updated understanding of Techstop in Noogler orientation assets

Scope

Owning and updating the Day 1 content , including content that is moved or added to post-orientation assets

Incorporating additional Techstop information into Day 2 and Day 3 content; subject to change at a later time

Content decisions

Does it keep Nooglers productive?

Is this topic applicable to all Googlers?

Does the topic help influence user behavior in a way that scales?

Is this hero content? (i.e. not instructional)

Does the content focus on the story - not the features?

Risks identified before launch

The content or approach may not resonate with Nooglers

Video may not be the best medium to convey the updated Techstop messaging.

Post-mortem surveys and metrics may reveal the new content not having a positive impact.

The content runs the risk of being lost in translation when undergoing localization efforts.

The content may need to be edited to reflect the different resources at each site.

What the pilot research changed

Selected findings from the retained user-experience study summary. The research summary’s author and sample size are not identified in this export. It is supporting research, not Mitchell’s sole-authored writing or independent proof of total program reach or customer satisfaction.

For both groups, what stood out most about the presentation is that Techstop is here to help Compared to the baseline group, what stood out to pilot participants is that there are many ways to get help and that self-service is an option

For those in the pilot group, the friendly tone and visuals shown in the video were strong communicators of their overall takeaway

The presenter and Techstop members onsite at orientation also impact their impression and key takeaways from the day
