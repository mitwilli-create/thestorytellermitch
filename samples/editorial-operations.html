The production procedure

Selected original passages, January 2018. Em dashes are normalized to commas; the team size is the author’s contemporaneous account.

As a Senior Producer at Al Jazeera International’s “AJ+,” it is my role to manage the team of 8-10 Real Time producers. This team produces the short-form, text on-screen videos that appear frequently in AJ+’s Facebook and Twitter feeds. I approve story ideas, greenlight production, approve the final product, and advise on the publishing schedule.

The planning producer, Facebook Live producer, newsgathering executive producer and engagement lead producer , all members of other departments , attend the morning meeting. We begin by recapping Doha’s production output and San Francisco videos ready for edit. Then, I handover to the four external producers as they outline coverage plans from their respective departments. Then, in either a clockwise or counterclockwise fashion, each individual producer is required to pitch at least three newsworthy stories. During this meeting, I listen for themes I know will resonate with our online audiences. I determine which ideas should be produced into shorter videos and which pitches need more time for development. I offer guidance, suggestions for interviews, and advice on social strategy. This meeting concludes with my assignments and deadlines. Those instructions are delivered via slack, and they include stories to produce that day, videos from yesterday that need to begin edit immediately, suggestions for our late shift producer, republishing of evergreen content, and suggested tweets.

For the pre-production process, I guide producers on where to find visuals We discuss the best ways to tell the story , keeping the current social conversation and AJ+ brand themes in mind. Each producer conducts their own research, interviews experts, writes the script and sends to a partner producer before I review. I copyedit and fact-check all scripts, and then I give the greenlight for production to begin. The scriptwriting and copyediting process takes place using Quip.

Producers are responsible for creating the first cut of the piece. They use photos, videos, GIFs, interviews, music and text to create the short-form videos AJ+ followers have become accustomed to seeing in their social media newsfeeds. The deadlines I establish correlate with the time that I need to view the rough edit of the piece. I screen the cut, instruct rewrites or image changes, and then I sign-off. At that point, the piece is handed over to a craft editor. The craft editor’s responsibilities include finessing the music, animating the text, adding video transitions, implement gradients, and color-correction.
