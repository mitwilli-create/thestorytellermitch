Resetting priorities.

Adapted from a retained transcript of a delivered address. Condensed for length and readability; identifying details generalized.

We made our annual plan before the world changed. We have now reviewed that plan, and our priorities need to change with it.

Some urgent work cannot wait. Other work remains essential because those priorities depend on it. This does not mean the work we planned at the start of the year was unimportant. It means we need to be clear about what must happen now and how we will support it with the resources we have.

Our priorities will continue to change as we learn more. I know that creates uncertainty. What should remain consistent are the principles behind our decisions.

First, we are one team. We all have a stake in these priorities, whether we work directly on them or support the systems and services that make them possible. Their success depends on work across the organization.

Second, we need to be good stewards of our resources. We will have to make choices, move people where they are needed, and adapt as the situation develops.

So what happens next? Please send the questions we have not answered. We will share more detail about the priorities and update our objectives and key results to reflect them.

I also know people will ask what this means for how their work is evaluated. Our conversations about performance need to reflect the updated priorities and each person's role in achieving them.

Thank you for your understanding and for working through more ambiguity than any of us would like. We still have important work to do, and we will need one another to do it. Take care of yourselves.

About this sample. This is a present-day portfolio edit, not the original delivered wording or a surviving authored draft.
